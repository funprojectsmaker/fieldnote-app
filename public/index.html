Features

Everything between "Can you come out?" and "Paid"

Replace the whiteboard, the group chat and the stack of paper invoices with one tool your techs will actually use.

Drag-and-drop scheduling

See every tech's day at a glance. Move a job and everyone is updated instantly.

Live tech tracking

Customers get an "on my way" text with a live map, so they stop calling the office.

Pay on the spot

Tap-to-pay on the tech's phone, or a payment link by text. Money lands next business day.

Quotes customers can approve

Send good / better / best options. Customers approve and e-sign from their phone.

Reports that make sense

Revenue per tech, close rate on quotes, and which jobs actually make you money.

Syncs with QuickBooks

Invoices and payments sync automatically, so your bookkeeper stays happy.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does setup take?

Most teams are scheduling real jobs within 30 minutes. Import your customer list from a spreadsheet or QuickBooks and invite your techs by text.

Do my techs need new phones?

No. Fieldnote runs on any iPhone or Android phone from the last five years, and works offline in basements and crawl spaces.

What do card payments cost?

2.7% + 15¢ per in-person tap, 2.9% + 30¢ for online links. No monthly payment fees and no long contracts.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel with one click. Yearly plans are refunded for unused months within the first 60 days.
